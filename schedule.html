Programme Schedule

November 26-27, 2026: a focused two-day outreach format combining psychoeducation, practical caregiver training, screening, counselling, community advocacy, and referral support.

Day 1 - Thursday, November 26, 2026

08:00 AM - 08:45 AM

Registration of Participants

Arrival, registration, and distribution of educational materials.

Speaker: Program Secretariat
09:00 AM - 09:10 AM

Opening Ceremony

Formal opening of the outreach program.

Speaker: CMD, Bowen University Teaching Hospital
09:10 AM - 09:20 AM

Welcome Address

Opening welcome from the organizing chairperson.

Speaker: Dr Titilayo Temitope Adeoye
09:20 AM - 09:30 AM

Overview of the Outreach Program

Theme introduction and objectives of the meeting.

Speaker: Secretary, Local Organizing Committee
09:30 AM - 10:15 AM

Session 1: The Basic Neuroscience of Movement Disorders

What happens in the brain, including basic neurophysiology and progression of movement disorders.

Facilitator: Prof Bamidele Victor Owoyele, Neuroscientist
10:15 AM - 11:00 AM

Session 2: Overview of Parkinson's Disease and Other Movement Disorders

Symptoms, progression, available treatment options, and the importance of multidisciplinary care.

Facilitator: Dr Nkechi Obianozie, Consultant Neurologist
11:00 AM - 11:30 AM

Tea Break / Networking

Refreshment and informal networking period.

Host: Welfare and Protocol Team
11:30 AM - 12:15 PM

Session 3: Mental Health and Movement Disorders: The Nexus

Depression, anxiety, psychosis, functional movement disorders, drug-induced movement disorders, cognitive symptoms, and help-seeking pathways.

Facilitator: Dr Titilayo Temitope Adeoye
12:15 PM - 01:00 PM

Session 4: Current Approaches to Diagnosis and Management

Current approaches to the diagnosis and management of Parkinson's disease in low-resource settings, followed by interactive discussion.

Facilitator: Dr Amos Ogirima Salami, Movement Disorder Specialist
01:00 PM - 02:00 PM

Lunch Break / Interactive Discussion / Networking

Lunch, informal discussion, and networking break.

Host: Welfare and Protocol Team
02:00 PM - 03:00 PM

Session 5: Caregiver Stress, Burnout, Self-Care, and Practical Caregiving Skills

Caregiver burden, coping strategies, resilience, medication adherence, nutrition, physical activity, communication, fall prevention, and home safety.

Facilitator: Dr Titilayo Temitope Adeoye
03:00 PM - 04:00 PM

Panel Discussion / Question and Answer Session

Theme: Caring Beyond the Clinic: The Challenges and Realities of Caregiving in Parkinson's Disease.

Panelists: Dr Amos Salami, Dr Nkechi Obianozie, Dr Titilayo Temitope Adeoye, Dr Oludolapo Oladeji, Mrs Adeleke, Mrs C. M Ajagbe, caregiver representative, food nutritionist, and social worker
04:00 PM - 04:30 PM

Presentation of Awards, Closing Remarks, and Group Photograph

Faculty appreciation, closing remarks, and group photograph.

Host: Local Organizing Committee

Theme: Promoting Community Awareness, Strengthening Community-Based Support for Caregivers and Reducing Stigma Around Movement Disorders.

09:00 AM - 09:30 AM

Courtesy Visit to Traditional Heads and Community Leaders

Community engagement visit in Ogbomoso town.

Participants: All faculty and planning committee members
09:30 AM - 11:30 AM

Community Advocacy Session

Recognising early warning signs and symptoms, reducing stigma, promoting early help-seeking, and sharing available local support resources.

Caregiver stress assessment, counselling, referrals, and individual consultations.

02:30 PM - 03:30 PM

Testimonies and Shared Experiences

Patients and caregivers share personal journeys of resilience and hope.

Moderator: Secretary, Local Organizing Committee
03:30 PM - 04:30 PM

Distribution of Educational Materials and Support Resources

Participants receive educational materials and support resources.

Host: Outreach Committee
04:30 PM - 04:45 PM

Closing Remarks and Appreciation

Closing reflection and appreciation from the chairperson of the Local Organizing Committee.

Speaker: Dr Titilayo Temitope Adeoye
04:45 PM - 05:00 PM

Group Photograph

Closing group photograph with participants, faculty, and organizing committee members.

Host: Media Team
