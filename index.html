About the program

Multidisciplinary care for people who support daily life with movement disorders.

Movement disorders such as Parkinson's disease impose substantial physical, psychological, social, and economic burdens on patients, caregivers, and families, especially in low-resource settings where access to specialist care and support services may be limited.

DWEP 2026 brings neuroscience, neurology, psychiatry, psychology, rehabilitation, nursing, nutrition, social support, counselling, and community advocacy into one practical outreach experience for caregivers and families in Ogbomoso.

The program aligns with the mission of the International Parkinson and Movement Disorder Society by advancing education, increasing awareness, supporting underserved populations, and improving access to multidisciplinary care in sub-Saharan Africa.

Highlights

Practical support across clinic and community settings.

Each session is built to help participants understand symptoms, caregiving realities, mental health needs, and available care pathways.

Neuroscience Education

Clear explanations of basic neurophysiology, Parkinson's disease, tremor, stiffness, gait changes, and progression.

Mental Health Support

Guidance on depression, anxiety, psychosis, cognitive changes, functional symptoms, stigma, and when to seek help.

Caregiver Training

Practical strategies for medication adherence, nutrition, physical activity, communication, fall prevention, and home safety.

Basic neurological, mental health, and social-needs screening with referral guidance.

Expected outcomes

What the outreach is designed to improve.

Structured education

50 participants will receive structured education on Parkinson's disease and other movement disorders.

Measured knowledge gain

Improved caregiver knowledge will be measured through pre- and post-program questionnaires.

Symptom awareness

Participants will better understand motor and non-motor symptoms of Parkinson's disease and other movement disorders.

Caregiver support

Caregivers experiencing significant burden, stress, anxiety, or depression will be identified for referral and support.

Home-based care

Participants will improve understanding of medication adherence, rehabilitation, fall prevention, and home-based care strategies.

Stigma reduction

The outreach will strengthen community awareness and reduce stigma toward people living with movement disorders.

Caregiver network

A local caregiver support network will be encouraged for ongoing peer support and information sharing.

Multidisciplinary collaboration

Collaboration among neurologists, psychiatrists, physiotherapists, nurses, psychologists, and community stakeholders will be strengthened.

Educational materials

Educational materials in English and Yoruba will support continued community education after the outreach program.
